======================
KEY NOTES
======================
- All WebP images use object-fit: contain, so they should be SQUARE (1:1) with transparent or matching background.
- 160x160 px covers the largest display (80x80 on cart page) at 2x retina density.
- Keep WebP images under 20 KB each for fast page loads (~180 KB total for all images).
- SVGs are resolution-independent, so exact pixel dimensions don't matter -- just keep file size small.
